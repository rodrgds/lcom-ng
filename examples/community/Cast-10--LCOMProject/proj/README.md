## Game Guide

### Controls

| Action | Player 1 | Player 2 |
|--------|----------|----------|
| Move Up | W | Arrow Up |
| Move Down | S | Arrow Down |
| Move Left | A | Arrow Left |
| Move Right | D | Arrow Right |
| Attack | Left Mouse Button | Right Mouse Button |
| Open/Close Shop | P | P |
| Exit | ESC | ESC |

### Shop

Press **P** during a match to open the shop. Spend your accumulated points to upgrade your champion:

- **Attack Damage** — increases the damage dealt per hit
- **Movement Speed** — increases how fast your champion moves
- **Max HP** — increases maximum health (also heals by the same amount)

Each upgrade costs **2 points**. Upgrades can be purchased multiple times up to a maximum level.

### Scoring

| Action | Points Earned |
|--------|---------------|
| Kill an enemy minion | +1 point |
| Kill the enemy champion | +2 points |
| Enemy killed by tower or minion | +2 points |

Accumulate points by fighting and use them in the shop to gain an edge over your opponent.
